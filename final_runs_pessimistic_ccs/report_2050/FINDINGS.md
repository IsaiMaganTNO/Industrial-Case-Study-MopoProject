## 1 Industry routes (`1_industry_routes_*`)
- Same in every run: chlorine electric; paper, pulp, secondary steel and aluminium on biomass/scrap; refinery hydrogen from gas reforming with capture (where NL+BE refine).
- NL+BE alone: ammonia on green hydrogen, primary steel mainly electric (17 zones: 71% electric, 14% H2, 14% gas DRI + capture; 1 node: 100% electric), glass on electricity/hydrogen, olefins partly from methanol made with captured CO2 (42% / 20%).
- With neighbours (all sensitivities): ammonia, primary steel, olefins and float glass switch to gas with capture, because CO2 can be exported for storage.
- National CO2 caps: close to "With neighbours"; slightly more hydrogen in glass (float 50%, fibre 65%).
- 10 repr. days: all glass to gas + capture (container, float); NL+BE refinery output moves to Denmark (see notes).
- Optimistic CCS: the most gas + capture (container, float and 65% of fibre glass).
- Cement: always oxy-fuel with capture; with neighbours partly or fully with biomass as fuel (shown as "biomass").

## 2 Electricity mix (`2_elecmix_*`)
- NL+BE alone: 413-423 TWh offshore wind and 53-96 TWh nuclear, driven by large electrolysis demand.
- With neighbours: offshore wind falls to ~246 TWh; new nuclear 6.0 GW in BE + 1.8 GW in NL.
- 10 repr. days: more offshore wind (311 TWh) and nuclear (95 TWh, NL 5.0 GW), plus large iron-air storage (see 4).
- Optimistic CCS: no NL+BE nuclear; gas power with capture instead (BE 88 TWh, NL 11 TWh) and less offshore wind (169 TWh).

## 3 Energy panels (`3_energy_*`)
- LH2 import is 42.5 TWh in every run (at its limit).
- Electrolytic hydrogen drops from 130 TWh (17 zones) to 9-46 TWh with neighbours.
- Hydrogen turbines only in the NL+BE-alone runs (23-24 TWh H2) and in 10 repr. days (11 TWh).

## 4 Hydrogen and electricity balance (`4_h2_elec_balance_*`)
- Hydrogen: no gas-based (SMR) hydrogen outside refineries. With neighbours, NL+BE are net hydrogen exporters (34-51 TWh), mostly the LH2 imports passed on.
- Electricity use 2050 (With neighbours): fixed demand 161, industry 149, electrolysis 45, building heat 12, storage 19, net export 41 TWh.
- 10 repr. days: 125 TWh into storage, of which 106 TWh iron-air batteries (434 units in NL, mostly NLC2). With 3 days the model builds no iron-air; with 10 days it values multi-day storage.
- Check: the use categories add up to within 2% of total supply (rest = network losses and small categories).

## 5 CO2 capture and destination (`5_co2_capture_dest_*`)
- NL storage (16 Mt) is full in every pessimistic run.
- NL+BE alone: the rest is used for methanol for plastics (12-16 Mt CCU), and net emissions are 0.
- With neighbours: no CCU; 35 Mt exported (mostly to the UK). NL+BE emit 90 Mt net, balanced by negative emissions in NO, UK and DK (shared cap).
- National CO2 caps: 63 Mt captured, 47 Mt exported. Net emissions = net export (47.1 Mt) because the 2050 cap is 0 and the exporter gets the credit.
- Optimistic CCS: 96 Mt captured, incl. 37 Mt from gas power plants; 27 Mt stored in NL and 69 Mt exported.
- 10 repr. days: less capture (36 Mt) because the NL refinery is not used.

## Notes on other countries (text only)
- **France (FR + LU run):** existing nuclear plants are retired after 40 years (assumption), leaving 14.7 / 4.8 / 1.7 GW in 2030 / 2040 / 2050.
- **Denmark:** in 10 repr. days, all NL+BE refinery hydrogen moves to DK gas reforming with capture (DK 1.38 vs 0.50 PWh in "With neighbours"). In the national caps run, DK refinery output falls and NL/BE output rises.
- **Optimistic CCS:** storage abroad is the main outlet (UK 164, NO 69, DE 60, DK 37 Mt in the whole model).
- **National CO2 caps:** DE becomes the largest exporter of CO2 (49 Mt), NL 30 and BE 24; NO, UK and DK host the storage.
- **Neighbours with negative emissions** (With neighbours, 2050): NO 53, UK 37, DK 10 Mt (biomass with capture and storage).
